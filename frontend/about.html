About the project

SignConnect AI

AI-Based Indian Sign Language Recognition and Communication Assistance System.

SignConnect AI is a web application that helps bridge the communication gap between people who use supported Indian Sign Language (ISL) hand signs and people who do not understand them. It uses a webcam, hand landmark detection and a trained machine learning model to turn signs into readable English text, which can then be spoken aloud.

Objectives

  • Recognize supported ISL alphabet signs from a live webcam.
  • Build words and sentences from confirmed letters.
  • Convert the sentence to speech in the browser.
  • Keep a private recognition history for each user.
  • Report real, honest model evaluation results.

Privacy

Camera frames are used only to detect a hand and predict a sign. They are not stored. Only the text you choose to save, with its date and duration, is kept in your history.

Known limitations

  • Alphabet recognition is not full sign language translation.
  • Signs that need movement, two hands, facial expression or body posture are not covered in version 1.
  • Accuracy depends on lighting, background, camera quality and the training dataset.
  • Results on a dataset can differ from real webcam performance.
