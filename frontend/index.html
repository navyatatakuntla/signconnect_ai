How it works

From a hand sign to spoken words

Four simple steps happen behind the scenes.

Capture

Your browser opens the webcam, with your permission, and sends small frames to the app.

Detect

MediaPipe locates your hand and extracts 21 landmarks (x, y, z), giving 63 numbers.

Recognize

A trained model predicts the sign. Low-confidence results are ignored.

Speak

You confirm letters, build a sentence, and the browser speaks it.

Important limitation: alphabet recognition is not full Indian Sign Language translation. Real sign language also uses hand movement, facial expressions, body posture and grammar, which version 1 does not cover.
