Tips for better results

  • Use good, even lighting and keep your hand clearly in view.
  • Show one hand at a time, facing the camera.
  • Hold each sign steady for a moment before adding the letter.
  • A plain background helps the hand detector.
  • Version 1 supports static alphabet signs only. Signs that need movement or two hands are not supported.
